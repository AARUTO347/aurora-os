# Code of Conduct

AURORA OS is a small project. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be direct about technical problems and respectful to the people discussing
  them.
- Critique code, not contributors.
- Respect privacy: do not publish someone else's logs, credentials, or
  personal information.
- Respect the project's credit policy: AURORA OS credits only AARUTO
  (Abhishek Solanki), DP-_-, and DEV.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
publishing private information, and deliberately disruptive conduct.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that violate
these rules. Report problems privately through the security contact in
`SECURITY.md`.
